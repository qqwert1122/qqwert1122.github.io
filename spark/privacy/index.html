SPARK Privacy Policy

luceforge studio ("we", "us") provides SPARK. This policy covers versions with optional usage analytics; earlier versions without analytics do not send these analytics events.

1. Optional usage analytics

Only after you consent, Google Analytics for Firebase (GA4) collects a random app-instance identifier, app opens, sessions and foreground usage time, metronome and trainer usage, tempo and meter, trainer input mode, Pro paywall views, and store-reported purchase events. The SDK also processes basic device/app information (model, operating system, app version and language) and approximate region derived from network information. We do not require an account or collect your name, email address, precise GPS location, advertising identifiers, microphone audio, detailed practice records, payment credentials or receipts through analytics. We do not use advertising tracking or cross-app profiles.

2. Microphone

The microphone is used only to detect drum hits in Rhythm training's Drums · Mic mode. Sound is analyzed on your device in real time and is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

3. Local data and Apple Watch

Presets, settings and detailed practice history stay on your device. Watch settings are exchanged only between your iPhone and Apple Watch. Optional analytics reports feature usage, not the contents of these records. Standalone Watch usage is not measured. Removing the app removes local app data, subject to your operating system's backup and restore settings; it does not automatically delete analytics already sent.

4. Purchases and support

App Store or Google Play handles payment. We do not receive card details. With analytics consent, Google may receive store-supplied purchase event metadata such as product, amount and currency. If you email us, your email and message are used to respond.

5. Purpose

We use analytics to measure daily, weekly and monthly active installations, new installations, sessions, engagement and feature usage, and to improve SPARK. Installation counts are estimates and are not a count of identifiable people.

6. Children

Optional analytics is not intended for children under 14. Children and guardians should leave it disabled. If you believe a child's data was collected, contact us to request investigation and deletion.

7. Providers, reports and international processing

After consent, analytics data is transmitted over encrypted connections during app use to Google LLC for GA4 processing. Google may process it in the United States and other countries where it operates. Only aggregated counts are sent to luceforge studio's private Slack channel for daily and weekly operational reports. Slack Technologies, LLC (Salesforce) processes those reports and may store them in the United States and other operating locations under the workspace's settings. Slack reports contain no app-instance identifiers, emails, audio or individual practice histories. Provider information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy and https://slack.com/trust/privacy/privacy-policy.

8. Retention

GA4 event-level data is retained for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months. Under the current GA4 setting, new activity can reset the user-level retention period. Aggregated standard reports can remain available beyond that period. Slack retains aggregated reports according to the workspace's retention settings until removed. Support emails are kept as necessary to handle the request and applicable legal obligations.

9. Choice, withdrawal and deletion

Collection is off by default and starts only after optional consent, including the international processing described above. Declining does not restrict any app feature. Change your choice at Settings > Help > Usage analytics. Turning it off stops future collection and resets analytics data and the app-instance identifier on your device; it does not automatically erase data already on Google's servers or aggregate Slack reports. You may request access, correction, deletion or restriction by contacting luceforge.studio@gmail.com. Because we keep no account or identity mapping, identifying past events may require information that can be obtained from your installation, and may not always be possible. We will explain what can be located and deleted; do not send audio, payment credentials or other unnecessary personal information.

10. Changes

We will announce material changes in the app or related information before applying them and obtain new consent when required.

Slack conserva los informes según los ajustes del espacio hasta que se eliminen. Los correos de soporte se conservan durante la atención y los plazos legales necesarios.
