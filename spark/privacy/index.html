SPARK Privacy Policy

luceforge studio ("we", "us") respects the privacy of people who use the SPARK app (the "app"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

1. Information we collect

The app does not collect any personal information, such as your name, email address, location or device identifiers. There is no account, and the app uses no advertising or analytics tools.

2. Microphone

The app uses the microphone only in the Drums · Mic mode of Rhythm training, to detect drum hits. Sound is analyzed on your device in real time; it is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

3. Information stored on your device

Presets, settings and practice history are stored only on your device. If you use Apple Watch, these settings pass only between your iPhone and your Apple Watch, never to our servers. Deleting the app deletes them.

If you buy a paid feature, the payment is handled by the App Store. We do not receive your payment information.

5. Contacting us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

6. Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

We will provide notice in the app or related information when this policy changes.
